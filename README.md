APIs for Workday's cloud-based recruiting and talent acquisition solution, providing programmatic access to job requisitions, candidate management, applications, interviews, job postings, and hiring workflows.

**Areas:** HCM · Human Resources · Recruiting · Software-as-a-Service · Talent Acquisition

## Workday Recruiting on APIs.io

Everything profiled for Workday Recruiting is published on APIs.io rather than in this repository. Start at the provider page — **[apis.io/providers/workday-recruiting/](https://apis.io/providers/workday-recruiting/)** — or go straight to one of the APIs or properties below.

### APIs (13)

- [Workday Recruiting SOAP Web Services API](https://apis.io/apis/workday-recruiting/workday-recruiting-soap-web-services-api/) — SOAP-based web service providing comprehensive access to Workday Recruiting business services data for integration with talent management and applicant…
- [Workday Recruiting Agent Definition API](https://apis.io/apis/workday-recruiting/workday-recruiting-agentdefinition-api/) — To view and maintain Agent Definitions with the Agent System of Record.
- [Workday Recruiting Applicants API](https://apis.io/apis/workday-recruiting/workday-recruiting-applicants-api/) — Retrieve and manage pre-hire and applicant records, including high-volume applicant import operations.
- [Workday Recruiting Background Checks API](https://apis.io/apis/workday-recruiting/workday-recruiting-background-checks-api/) — Submit and retrieve background check results and manage background check packages for candidate screening.
- [Workday Recruiting Candidates API](https://apis.io/apis/workday-recruiting/workday-recruiting-candidates-api/) — Create, retrieve, and update candidate profiles including personal information, attachments, photos, and assessment data.
- [Workday Recruiting Configuration API](https://apis.io/apis/workday-recruiting/workday-recruiting-configuration-api/) — Manage recruiting configuration settings including questionnaires, assessment categories, veteran statuses, site brands, and regions.
- [Workday Recruiting Evergreen Requisitions API](https://apis.io/apis/workday-recruiting/workday-recruiting-evergreen-requisitions-api/) — Manage evergreen requisitions used for ongoing hiring needs without a specific number of openings or close date.
- [Workday Recruiting Interviews API](https://apis.io/apis/workday-recruiting/workday-recruiting-interviews-api/) — Schedule interviews, submit interview feedback, manage interview settings, and configure self-schedule calendars.
- [Workday Recruiting Job Applications API](https://apis.io/apis/workday-recruiting/workday-recruiting-job-applications-api/) — Manage the lifecycle of job applications from submission through disposition, including stage movement and offer initiation.
- [Workday Recruiting Job Postings API](https://apis.io/apis/workday-recruiting/workday-recruiting-job-postings-api/) — Post, update, and unpost jobs to internal and external career sites, and manage job posting site configurations.
- [Workday Recruiting Job Requisitions API](https://apis.io/apis/workday-recruiting/workday-recruiting-job-requisitions-api/) — Create, retrieve, edit, close, freeze, and manage job requisitions for open positions within Workday recruiting workflows.
- [Workday Recruiting Positions API](https://apis.io/apis/workday-recruiting/workday-recruiting-positions-api/) — Create and manage positions and position restrictions within the position management staffing model.
- [Workday Recruiting Agencies API](https://apis.io/apis/workday-recruiting/workday-recruiting-recruiting-agencies-api/) — Manage recruiting agency relationships, agency users, and agency candidate submissions.

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Workday Recruiting: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.

- **A new API, or one that is not in the catalog yet** — [submit it on APIs.io](https://apis.io/add/).
- **Anything else** — [the APIs.io inbox](https://github.com/api-search/inbox/issues/new) or [info@apievangelist.com](mailto:info@apievangelist.com).
